# Product Guidelines

## Brand Messaging & Tone
- **Primary Tone:** Professional and Reliable. We emphasize the "Verified Vibe" to build trust and security.
- **Secondary Tone:** Energetic and Exciting. We celebrate the "Spark Your Moment" aspect to keep the experience engaging.

## Visual Identity & Aesthetic
- **Cute & Friendly:** Approachable and welcoming design elements to reduce friction.
- **Modern & Premium:** A clean, high-contrast, and sophisticated interface.
- **Trustworthy & Safe:** Design choices that reinforce security and clarity.

## Design Principles
- **Consistency:** Rigorous adherence to the shared UI package (`minglit_kit`) and design tokens.
- **Accessibility:** Ensuring high readability and usability for a diverse user base.
- **Feedback:** Immediate and meaningful visual responses to all user interactions.
- **Professionalism & Ease of Use:** A UI that looks professional to instill reliability while remaining intuitive and simple to navigate.

## Key Attributes
- **Name:** Minglit (Mingle + Lit)
- **Slogan:** Verified Vibe, Spark Your Moment
- **Core Values:** Trust, Safety, Quality, Excitement, Cute
